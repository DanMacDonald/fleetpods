# Glossary

**Seat**: The one holder of playback among this machine, fleet peers, and Apple devices. Claimed, yielded, or idle. Only the seat module changes who holds it.

**Attempt**: This machine's one in-flight connection attempt, typed by Model as waiting, connecting, reconnecting, or switching (or none). The panel's trace, seats, and ring render from it; its label is display-only.

**Pod placement**: An individual pod's known position inside or outside its charging well. Unknown placement is not a departure or a return; wearing a pod proves it is outside the well.

**Handoff**: A transfer of playback away from the current Seat holder. Local playback may still have queued audio to drain after its players pause.

**Preferred connector**: The fleet device with the most recent activity, remembered while the AirPods are stowed. Only this device reconnects automatically when they become available. Sleep or absence does not transfer the preference; fresh activity or an explicit Connect does.
